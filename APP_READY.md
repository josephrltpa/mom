## 📱 How to Use

### For Your Mom:

1. **Open the app** on her phone/browser
2. **Tap her name** at the top to change it to her actual name
3. **Add her medications** (tap the + button next to "Medications")
4. **Log daily vitals** (tap the BP or Sugar cards)
5. **Mark medicines as taken** (tap the ✓ button)

### Everything is Editable:

- ✅ Mom's name and details
- ✅ All medications (add/edit/delete)
- ✅ Doctor appointments
- ✅ Lab reports and prescriptions
- ✅ Daily vitals (BP, blood sugar)

---

## 💾 Data Storage

All data is saved in the browser's localStorage:
- ✅ Works offline
- ✅ Data persists between sessions
- ✅ Private (stays on the device)

**Note:** If you clear browser data, the data will be reset. For cloud backup, we can add Supabase sync later (optional).
