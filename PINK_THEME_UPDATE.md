## 🎯 How to Use

### View Past Readings:
1. Go to **Records** tab (📋 icon)
2. Tap **Vitals** sub-tab
3. Scroll down to see **ALL** your readings
4. Each reading shows date, time, value, and status
5. Color-coded for easy understanding

### View Charts:
1. Go to **Home** tab (🏠 icon)
2. Scroll down to see charts
3. **BP Chart** - Tap "7 Days", "30 Days", or "All Time"
4. **Sugar Chart** - Tap "7 Days", "30 Days", or "All Time"
5. See trends over time!

### Export for Doctor:
1. Go to **Records** tab (📋 icon)
2. Tap **Vitals** sub-tab
3. See big pink "Export for Doctor" section at top
4. Tap **CSV**, **Text**, or **Share**
5. Send to doctor!
